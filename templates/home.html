How to read a score

Three components, one 0 to 100 score

  • E
    Exposure

    How strongly the place is exposed to climate hazards such as drought, flood, and heat.

  • S
    Sensitivity

    How much women's daily work and income depend on the resource under pressure.

  • A
    Adaptive capacity

    Access and assets that help women cope. Higher capacity lowers the hotspot score.

0Very low 20Low 40Medium 60High 75Very high
